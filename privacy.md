# Privacy Policy for Word Vault

**Effective date:** 5 September 2026
**Last updated:** 5 September 2026

Word Vault is a vocabulary app published by Azizbek Khamidov
("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use
it. It applies to the Word Vault mobile app on Android and iOS.

## The short version

Word Vault has no accounts and no servers. Everything you create in the app —
your words, your categories, your study history, your name and avatar — is
stored **only on your device** and is never sent to us. We cannot see it, we
cannot recover it, and we do not have a copy.

The one exception is advertising. Word Vault shows ads from Google AdMob, and
Google collects information in order to serve them. That is the only data
collection that happens, and this policy describes it below.

## What stays on your device

The app stores the following in a private database on your phone, inside the
app's own storage area:

- The words you save — the term, its translations and definitions, notes,
  which category it is in and which language it is tagged with
- The categories you create
- Your study history: each learn session, the answers you gave, and your daily
  progress against your goal
- Your profile — the display name and avatar you choose, your daily goal, and
  your streak record
- Your settings: theme, interface language, selected content language, and
  study reminder time

**None of this is transmitted anywhere.** The app makes no network request to
retrieve or store any of it. There is no sign-in, no account, no user
identifier, and no server belonging to us.

Because there is no server, there is also no backup held by us. If you delete
the app or lose the device, this data is gone. The app includes an export
feature so you can save a copy of it yourself — see "Backups and files" below.

## What we do not do

- We do not ask for or store an email address, phone number or password.
- We do not use analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
- We do not use crash reporting.
- We do not sell or share your vocabulary data with anyone, because we never
  receive it.
- We do not track your location.
- We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

## Advertising

Word Vault is free and supported by ads. Ads are provided by **Google AdMob**.
To serve them, Google may collect and process:

- A device advertising identifier (the Android Advertising ID, or on iOS the
  Identifier for Advertisers, where you have permitted it)
- Approximate location, derived from your IP address
- Device and app information, such as device model and operating system version
- Information about the ads you are shown and interact with

This processing is carried out by Google, not by us, and is governed by
Google's own policies. You can read how Google uses this data here:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

**Your choices about ads:**

- In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Word Vault shows
  Google's consent form the first time you open the app, and your choice
  determines whether you see personalised or non-personalised ads. You can
  change that choice at any time from within the app.
- On iOS, the app asks for permission to track before any tracking identifier
  is used. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be
  non-personalised.
- On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device
  settings under Privacy → Ads.
- Subscribing to Word Vault Premium removes all ads, and with them this data
  collection.

## Subscriptions

Word Vault offers an optional premium subscription. Purchases are handled
entirely by the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play**, depending on your
device. We never see or receive your payment details, billing address, or the
identity of the account that made the purchase.

The app asks the store whether a valid subscription exists and stores only that
yes-or-no answer on the device, so that premium features work when you are
offline. Nothing about the purchase leaves your device by way of the app.

Refunds, cancellations and billing questions are handled by Apple or Google
under their terms, not by us.

## Study reminders

If you turn on study reminders, the app schedules a notification on your device
at the time you pick. These are **local notifications**: they are created and
delivered by your phone. There is no push service, no notification token, and
no server involved. The reminder time is stored on your device with your other
settings.

## Backups and files

The app can export your vault as a file, and can import words from a file you
choose.

- **Export** writes a file and hands it to your operating system's share
  sheet. Where it then goes — a messaging app, cloud storage, your own
  computer — is entirely your choice and is governed by whatever service you
  send it to. We never receive it.
- **Import** reads a file you select with your device's document picker. The
  app reads only the file you pick, and only when you pick it.

## Permissions the app requests

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet | Only for advertising, the subscription store, and the ad consent form. No app data is sent or fetched. |
| Notifications | Only if you turn on study reminders. |
| Run at startup | So a scheduled reminder still arrives after you restart your phone. |
| Vibration | Haptic feedback while using the app. |

## Children

Word Vault is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age
in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from
children. Because the app collects no personal information at all, and shows
ads under the settings described above, parents concerned about advertising
should be aware that the premium subscription removes ads entirely.

## Your rights and how to exercise them

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose,
correct, export or delete on your behalf. You are in direct control of
everything the app holds:

- **To see your data:** it is all visible in the app, and the export feature
  produces a complete, readable copy.
- **To delete your data:** uninstalling the app removes the database and all
  settings from your device. You can also delete individual words, categories
  and your profile from within the app.
- **Advertising data** held by Google is subject to Google's policies, linked
  above, and to the controls described in the Advertising section.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page
and change the "Last updated" date. Material changes will be noted in the app's
release notes on the store listing.
